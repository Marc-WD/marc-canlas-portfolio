PanlasaTrip: A Modern WordPress Food Blog

PanlasaTrip is a WordPress-based blog platform dedicated to exploring and showcasing traditional Filipino frozen desserts. The website provides curated articles, detailed recipe guides, and cultural insights into popular treats such as Halo-Halo, Buko Pandan, and Leche Flan Ice Cream.

Built using the WordPress Content Management System (CMS), the platform allows efficient content organization, category management, and user interaction through comments. The design focuses on clean layout structure, strong visual storytelling, and an engaging reading experience.
